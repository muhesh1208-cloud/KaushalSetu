Understanding Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)

Under the Government of India's Skill India Mission and PMKVY, RPL acknowledges competencies acquired through unorganized work experience, self-learning, and apprenticeship.

🎖️

Formal NSQF Alignment

Transforms informal hands-on expertise into standardized National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) levels, recognized across government and private sectors.

📈

Dignity & Higher Wages

Certified tradespeople command 20% to 35% higher daily wages, gain credibility with organized contractors, and qualify for overseas employment visas.

🎯

Targeted Bridge Training

Instead of forcing experienced artisans into 6-month generic training, RPL identifies exact skill gaps (e.g. digital testing or safety codes) for short 12-to-30 hour bridge courses.

End-to-End Workflow

How KaushalSetu Operates

A seamless 4-step pipeline designed for simplicity, transparency, and assessor accountability.

1

Worker Registration

Candidate registers their basic profile, years of informal experience, preferred local language, and vocational trade with zero paperwork.

2

Experience Narrative

Worker describes their daily tasks, tools handled, and safety practices via text or hands-free voice input in their regional language.

3

Explainable AI Mapping

AI matches the narrative against official Qualification Pack criteria, extracting concrete evidence sentences and highlighting competencies with complete transparency.

4

Assessor Practical Exam

Certified Sector Assessor reviews AI insights, conducts hands-on checklist tests and viva, and authorizes the official RPL Skill Report.

Key Innovations

Features Built for True Ground Impact

Addressing India's unorganized workforce challenges through intelligent, accessible technology.

🎙️

Voice-Enabled Narrative Input

Informal workers may lack formal typing skills. KaushalSetu allows artisans to speak freely about their work using browser speech recognition, ready for Gemini multimodal audio expansion.

🔍

Zero Black-Box: Explainable AI

Unlike opaque classifiers, our engine explicitly highlights which tools, safety protocols, and performance criteria were verified, along with extracted text snippets.

⚖️

Human-in-the-Loop Assessor Control

Complies with statutory ethical standards. AI suggestions never replace assessors; certified assessors can override, adjust scores, and hold sole authority over certification.

📄

Instant Printable RPL Skill Transcript

Generates standardized, tamper-evident skill reports detailing demonstrated competencies, identified skill gaps, and assessor remarks ready for PDF export or hardcopy printing.
